Species Extinction In New Zealand

Extinction Of Kiwis

Kiwis are the second most endangered species in the world with only around 68,000 left. There population has been decreasing by about 2 precent every year which is roughly 20 kiwis per week. At this rate, kiwis will be extinct in the next 50 years. Kiwis are an extremly valuble to New Zealand as they are a large part of our cultural identity and seen by Maori as a taonga. There are many reasons why kiwis population is declining quickly such as habitat loss caused by humans making space for building and farm land, being attacked by stoats, ferrets, cats and dogs. They are also more vulnerable to crushing injurys, such as dog bites, as they have under developed wings, chest muscles and no breastbone.
