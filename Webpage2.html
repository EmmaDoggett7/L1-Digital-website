Kaitiakitanga

How you can be a kaitiaki

What is Kaitiakitanga?

Kaitiakitanga is a Maori word that means guardianship and protection. It is the responsibility
New Zealand has to protect and nurture the land, sky, sea and resourses for the future generations
This steams from the belief that humans are linked to the natural world, so we are responsible
for maintaining natural balance. It is a mutual relationship between the land taking care of us
and us taking care of the land. Kaitiakitanga is important as it protects the natural world such as
our land, rivers and native species, rather then allowing them to be exploited and destroyed. It
ensures that resources are passed down to future generations in a healthy state, allowing gaurdianship
to be continued. It also reinforced the connection between Maori and the environment, strengthening
cultural identity, and restoring/protecting natural sites. Us taking care of the environment means that
the environment will take care of us in return. This has been a very important part of Maori culture for
many years, it is very important that we keep up this tradition and ideal as it is beneficial for everyone
in NZ. Many other countrys follow similar ideaks of kaitiakitanga as well, so everyone is collectively
protecting earths enviroment.

How To Be A Kaitiaki For Kiwis

There are many ways that you can help protect kiwis as a part of kaitiakitanga. Dogs are a common
predator to kiwis, so keep them contained on your property and leashed during walks near forests.
If you go hunting with your dog, make sure they are trained with a kiwi adversion permit to prevent them
from attacking kiwis. Keeping cats inside will also help kiwis, as cats are yet another common predator.
Reporting stray dogs near forests also prevent kiwis being attacked, as people will be able to bring the
dog to the spca or elsewhere to ensure the kiwis are safe. If driving near kiwi habitat, which is indicated
with a yellow kiwi sign on the road, make sure you are driving carefully and looking out for kiwis. If you
spot an injured kiwi, you should take them to the vet as it will be free of charge (discuss with DOC). You
can transport the kiwi in a ventilated box, but make sure to store it in a cool and secure place, avoiding
loud noises. If you find a dead kiwi, you should take a photo of where you found it and report it. Putting
it into a bag and keeping in the fridge will give the DOC important information as to what killed it,
allowing other kiwis to be protected from the same issue. These steps may seem quite simple and like they
may not make that big of an impact on the kiwi population, however they do. This allows kiwis to have a safe
habitat to live in and breed at. It also allows professionals with wildlife to get a better understanding on
what is threatening the kiwi population so they can try prevent it to keep them safe. If you want to help even
more to protect kiwis, there are many help organisations and charitys in New Zealand that focus on looking after
and growing the kiwi pouplation. These organisations have had great progess with their efforts, so joining them
or atleast helping by donating allows them to further protect and help kiwis.

Help Organisations

Department Of Conservation
DOC a good website to check out if you wish to learn more information about how to keep kiwis, other animals,
and the rest of the environment in New Zealand safe and protected. This is expecially important to check out if
you go hunting, fishing, or camping as it provides useful tips on how to be prepared and how you can have fun
and take care of the enviroment at the same time.

Save The Kiwi
This organisation work alongside many iwi, conservation groups and communitys to reverse the decline of kiwi population. They do
this by creating more habitats for kiwis and bringing awareness to their endangerment and importance to New Zealand.
They also run a kiwi breeding and repopulation program to help further increase the population of them. This can be
a good organisation to join as they work with communitys and you are able to help in simple ways such as donating,
participating in fundrasiers and buying items from their store.

Project Kiwi
Project Kiwi has been managing kiwi conservation since 1996. They use methods such as pest and predator management to help protect
and restore kiwi population. They have also invested in habitat and biodiversity inventory to provide more space for
kiwi habitat. There are many ways to support their organisation, such as buying them predator traps, donating, and even
partnering with them if you are part of an enviromental organisation yourself.
